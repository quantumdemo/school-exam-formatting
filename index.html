About the Project

Purpose & Mission

Secondary school teachers often spend hours manually formatting examination papers to meet strict school standards. The Exam Formatter was created to solve this specific pain point.

Our mission is to empower educators by automating the tedious task of layouting, spacing, and numbering. By pasting raw questions into this tool, teachers can generate a perfectly structured document in seconds, allowing them to focus more on content quality and student engagement.

"Empowering Teachers Through Technology"

This initiative was brought to life through the innovative idea of Afeez Alimi Olalekan, who recognized the need for a simplified document generation tool tailored for the African academic environment, specifically matching standard WAEC formatting.
